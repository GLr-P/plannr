Everything you keep track of, in one calm app

Notes, a calendar, customers, tickets, money and a locked vault. A free, open-source organizer for Windows. Your data stays on your computer.

Download for Windows View on GitHub
Windows 10 and 11 · Free under GPL-3.0 · No account, no subscription

One place instead of five apps

For freelancers, small businesses, side projects or just your own life. Use what you need; hide the rest.

Notes

Checklists, tables, callouts, toggles and photos. Link anything with @. Folders, your own sections, and templates for meetings, plans, logs and more.

Calendar

Month, week and day views. Drag notes and tickets onto a day. Windows reminders, and public holidays built in.

Customers

The people and companies you work with, and everything you’ve done for them. Find anyone by name, email or phone number in any format.

Tickets

Track jobs, repairs, orders or requests from start to finish: status, price, photos and a fill-in form you design. Print slips, receipts and labels.

Money

Income with tax included or on top, expenses, bills and subscriptions with auto-pay, and who still owes you. CSV export.

Vault

Passwords, cards and documents encrypted with your own passcode (AES-256). Locks itself when you step away.

Calm, fast, and out of your way

A clean layout with light and dark modes. Search everything with Ctrl K.

Connects to what you already use (optional)

Each connection uses your own free developer key, so your data goes straight from your computer to the service. Step-by-step instructions are built in.

Google Calendar

Plannr events on your phone; your Google calendars inside Plannr.

Zoho Mail

Every email with a customer, right on their page.

QuickBooks Online

Customers, payments and expenses go to your books automatically.

Install in a minute

  1. Download Plannr-Setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Run it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway (the installer isn’t code-signed yet).
  3. Plannr opens with a short welcome tour: your name, what you want to use, and a few tips. After that it keeps itself up to date.

Your data lives on your computer and is backed up every day to Documents\Plannr Backups. No account, no server, no tracking.
